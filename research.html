THE RESEARCH PROCESS

Every market view
needs its context.

A disciplined research process helps traders assess the source and strength of an observation rather than assuming a signal is certain.

01

Check market data

Verify symbol, instrument type, exchange and candle history before analysis.

02

Review technical structure

Use indicators alongside actual price action; no indicator predicts the future with certainty.

03

Consider economic events

Account for scheduled releases and news when verified sources are available.

04

Apply independent risk limits

Position sizing, stops and trading decisions remain the user's responsibility.

RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

Not every data point
is a tradable price.

Some crypto sources are spot-reference feeds. Forex may show dated indicative rates when intraday history is unavailable. We label these distinctly and don't claim live historical performance without verified backtesting.
